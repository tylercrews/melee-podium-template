do we calculate the seeds based on what we're observing from the bracket? Or for a large tournament with pools will we get seed data from the overall tournament?






7:03 PM






We don’t calculate seeds ourselves. We pass through Start.gg’s initialSeedNum for each entrant.
For a phase-group link, seeds come from the entrants in that selected phase’s standings response. Whether that represents the overall event seed or a pool/phase-specific seed depends on what Start.gg provides for that phase; our code does not currently fetch or reconcile a separate event-wide seeding list.
So: placements are phase-specific, and seeds are provider-supplied—not inferred.
