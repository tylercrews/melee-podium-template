# Historic and Average Melee Rankings

Source tables checked on October 13, 2026. This document covers the annual global Melee Top 100 lists from 2013 through 2025, plus Summer 2026 as **Current rankings**.

## Scoring and scope

- **Score for each listed player:** `101 - published rank`. First place earns 100 points; 100th place earns 1 point. These are calculated points, not the source tables' panel ratings.
- **Annual editions included:** 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025. The 2018 and 2019 lists use the MPGR name, the successor to SSBMRank. [Series history](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Melee_Panda_Global_Rankings).
- **2020 and 2021:** no annual global Top 100 list was published. They contribute no edition or points. The 2021 MPGRContenders tier list is excluded because it does not supply individual numeric ranks. [MPGR history](https://www.ssbwiki.com/Melee_Panda_Global_Rankings), [PGRContenders](https://www.ssbwiki.com/PGRContenders).
- **Summer editions:** all are excluded except Summer 2026, which counts once in the total and is labeled Current rankings. It published only a Top 30; ranks 31-100 are not available and are not invented. [Summer 2026](https://www.ssbwiki.com/SSBMRank_Summer_2026).
- **Unlisted players:** receive 0 points for that edition, including anyone outside the published Current rankings Top 30. Honorable mentions and Mr. 101 entries receive no points.
- **Ties:** preserve the published rank and award equal points. The 2013 list ties players at ranks 34, 36, 42, 85, and 87, skipping the following ranks. Its 100 entries therefore total 5,055 points; each other annual list totals 5,050. [2013 table](https://www.ssbwiki.com/SSBMRank_2013#Rankings).
- **Player identities:** combine aliases through SmashWiki's canonical player pages and redirects. Annual tables keep the tags displayed in their source; the scoreboard uses the most recent tag appearing in an included table.
- **Scoreboard:** sum points across all 12 included editions (11 annual lists plus Current rankings). Sort by total descending; equal totals share a scoreboard rank and are displayed alphabetically by tag. Exactly 50 players are shown.
- **Average score:** total score divided by all 12 included editions, counting an unlisted edition as 0. **Average listed rank:** arithmetic mean of the published ranks only in editions where that player appears. Both averages are rounded to two decimal places. This scoring rewards longevity; the shorter Current rankings list gives points only to its Top 30.
